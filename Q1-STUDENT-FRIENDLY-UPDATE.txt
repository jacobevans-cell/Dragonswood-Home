Q1 CURRICULUM QUEST — STUDENT-FRIENDLY ACCOUNTABILITY UPDATE

Changes:
1. Removed freely clickable Watch/Practice/Apply/Master progression.
2. Required videos now open inside Dragonswood.
3. Watch only turns green after:
   - the lesson stays open for at least 60 seconds, AND
   - the student submits a one-sentence video check.
4. Practice is locked until the video check is complete.
5. Practice requires written evidence before it turns green.
6. Teacher Verify is hidden from students and only appears for the authorized Dragonswood teacher Firebase account.
7. Student grade is pulled from the existing students/{uid}.grade profile when available.
8. Student-facing cards use short, kid-friendly titles and directions.
9. Original pacing-guide wording is hidden from students and available to the teacher under source details.
10. Opening-day housekeeping items (welcome/routines/procedures/rules/expectations) from Days 1–3 are filtered out; academic Day 3 content remains.
11. Where the source guide lists an 'In Class' task, that task is now shown as the required activity.
12. Morphology cards use the morphology sequence's application task when available.

Technical note:
Because the Google video player does not expose reliable cross-origin playback telemetry to this page,
the system cannot mathematically prove every second was played. It therefore uses the strongest
practical browser-side check available here: in-app viewing time plus a required video reflection.
