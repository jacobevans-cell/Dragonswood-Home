Why PhET?
This is the University of Colorado's open-source My Solar System simulation. Students can manipulate masses, velocities, positions, paths, gravity-force vectors, center of mass, and preset orbital systems instead of relying on our homemade imitation.

🔬 Science Guide

🧲 Gravity
Every mass attracts every other mass. More mass means stronger gravitational attraction. Greater distance weakens gravity dramatically because gravitational force follows an inverse-square relationship.
➡️ Velocity
An orbit happens because a body is moving sideways while gravity continually bends its path inward. Too little speed can lead to a collision. Enough speed can create an orbit. Sufficient speed can produce an escape trajectory.
⚖️ Center of Mass
Two bodies both orbit their shared center of mass. A star does not sit perfectly still while a planet moves around it.
☄️ Ellipse vs. Hyperbola
A closed elliptical path is a bound orbit. A hyperbolic path is unbound, meaning the object passes through the system and escapes.
🪐 Multi-body Systems
Adding more objects makes gravity more complicated. Worlds can perturb one another, collide, exchange energy, or even slingshot objects onto new trajectories.
