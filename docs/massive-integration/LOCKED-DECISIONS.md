## Arcade Tokens

- Arcade Tokens are free-time access currency, not game rewards.
- Wallet cap: 3 Tokens.
- Students may earn up to 3 Tokens in an eligible class period.
- The three checkpoints are Ready, Responsible and Complete; each checkpoint is worth at most one Token per eligible period.
- Partial balances carry until spent, but the wallet can never exceed 3.
- Spending all 3 Tokens starts one 30-minute Arcade session.
- Students may earn another 3 after spending; they cannot stockpile beyond 3.
- Arcade access requires both a full wallet and teacher Arcade Time activation.
- Teacher activation may be whole-class or individual.
- Direct Arcade URLs, refreshes, tabs and device changes must enforce the same gate and authoritative expiration.
- Game performance never awards Arcade Tokens, Gold, XP, class points or direct Dragonswood rewards.
- Leaderboard eligibility remains teacher-reviewed under the existing Arcade rules.
- Teacher technical refunds are supported and auditable.
