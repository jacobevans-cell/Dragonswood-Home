# Rune Spelling Spalding phonogram audio

The active preview bridge bank uses Jacob's authorized five-track Spalding phonogram package. All 87 numbered phonograms were inventoried and split into 127 ordered sound clips. General American bridge IPA is routed to an exact matching Spalding sound; compound bridge chunks play those exact sounds in order.

The source-to-clip timestamps, checksums, transformations, and active IPA routes are recorded in `phoneme-audio-manifest.json`. The MIT bank remains as a local rollback but is not routed by the rebuilt preview.
