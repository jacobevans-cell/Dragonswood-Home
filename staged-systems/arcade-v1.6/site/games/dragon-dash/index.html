How to Play

Jump / action: Space, ↑, W, mouse click, or touch.

Cube: press to jump. Ship: hold to rise. Ball: press to flip gravity. UFO: each press flaps. Wave: hold to climb diagonally. Robot: hold for a taller jump. Spider: press to snap to the opposite surface. Swing: press to alternate gravity and arc through the passage.

New v1.4 mechanics: Mini portals shrink the dragon, linked portals teleport, reverse runes change travel direction, slopes and moving/rotating platforms change the terrain, and dash/spider/teleport orbs create advanced routes.

Practice: checkpoints are created automatically as you advance. Press C to drop one manually and Backspace to remove the newest checkpoint.

Restart: R. Pause: Escape or P.
