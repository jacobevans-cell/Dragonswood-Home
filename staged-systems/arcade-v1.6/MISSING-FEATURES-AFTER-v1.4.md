# Missing Features After v1.4

## Dragon Dash — now primarily difficult / large systems

The normal/medium pass now covers Mini, Swing, linked teleportation, slopes, moving/orbiting platforms, dash/gravity-dash/spider/teleport orbs, reverse travel, free-fly sections, groups, selection, layers, Move/Rotate/Spawn triggers, camera zoom/offset, metadata, and optional custom music.

### Still missing or deliberately deferred

1. **Dual Mode** — two independently colliding forms controlled simultaneously.
2. **Platformer Mode** — full manual left/right movement, friction, platformer checkpoints, camera rules, and platformer-specific gameplay.
3. **Complete 2.2 trigger language** — Follow, Count, Collision, Pickup, Random/Advanced Random, TimeWarp, Player Control, area/event triggers, and the many specialized modern triggers.
4. **Keyframe animation system** — keyframes, timelines, easing, and grouped interpolation tooling.
5. **Advanced camera system** — static camera, camera edges, full camera rotation, free camera, follow variants, and all camera trigger options.
6. **Shader/post-processing system** — glitch, pixelate, chromatic aberration, blur, shockwave, bulge, hue effects, split screen, etc.
7. **True rotated-surface collision** for arbitrary rotating platforms/groups. v1.4 has orbiting/rotating platforms with simplified dynamic collision.
8. **Full online level ecosystem** — upload, browse, search, ratings, likes/comments, moderation, versioning, featured/event content.
9. **Large shared icon/cosmetic library** — better implemented later as Dragonswood Arcade dragon cosmetics rather than a separate Geometry Dash economy.
10. **Full official-level/content volume** — no copyrighted Geometry Dash levels, music, artwork, or branding are bundled.

## Void Runner — difficult systems remaining from v1.3

1. True freestanding gravity-aware 3D boxes.
2. Duplicator-style independently simulated clones.
3. Light-bridge dragon that creates temporary physical platforms.
4. Bridge Building-style movable-box puzzle campaign.
5. Full Void Runner level editor.
6. Hundreds-level handcrafted campaign/content volume.
7. Larger cinematic/story system.

## Reward boundary remains unchanged

Arcade games record performance only. They do not directly write Dragonswood Gold, XP, class points, pets, fragments, or other reward totals. Leaderboard qualification remains the only reward-eligibility bridge.
