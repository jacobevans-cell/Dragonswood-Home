## Contributor record required for future art

Before new mascot art becomes official, record:

1. Contributor or generation source.
2. Date created or received.
3. Prompt/brief or commission scope when available.
4. Permission and redistribution terms.
5. Source file location.
6. Derived export paths.
7. Reviewer and approval date.
8. Light/dark proof result and automated verification result.

## Approval rule

Only assets listed in `mascot.manifest.json` and `ASSET-CATALOG.csv` are approved production assets. References, atlases, proofs, temporary files, and unlisted experiments are not approved for student-facing use.
