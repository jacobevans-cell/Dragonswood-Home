HOW TO PLAY

Three buttons. A suspicious amount of falling.

ASTRAL SHARDS

Collect shards to unlock alternate dragons inside this game. They are game progression only, not Dragonswood rewards.

FRAGILE TILES

They crack underfoot and connected fragile panels can collapse in a chain. Cloudwing is light enough not to break them.

❄

ICE RUNES

Ice boosts forward momentum but makes steering slippery. Level 5, Thin Ice, now teaches this mechanic directly.

⇆

FLOW RUNES

Green speeds you up, orange slows you down, and blue or purple panels push you left or right.

LOW POWER

Later passages dim and flicker. Bright gold rune panels stay visible as route clues while the music becomes sparse.

CHANGING TUNNELS

Explore routes now use different side counts, widths, and lane counts. A square corridor turns 90° at an edge; a 16-sided ring rotates in much smaller steps.

╱╲

RAMPS

Runic routes include real raised ramp pairs. Ride the slope up and back down while keeping your lane centered.

DRAGON ABILITIES

Runewing rotates gravity with Q/E. Skywing air-dashes with Shift/X and glides while the ability control is held. Touch devices get an ability rune.
