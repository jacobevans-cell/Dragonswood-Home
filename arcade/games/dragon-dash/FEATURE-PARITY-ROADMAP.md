# Dragon Dash Feature Parity Roadmap

## Completed through Arcade v1.4

Cube, Ship, Ball, UFO, Wave, Robot, Spider, Swing, Mini/full size, gravity and five speed tiers, linked teleport portals, reverse travel, free-fly sections, yellow/pink/red/blue/green/black/dash/gravity-dash/spider/teleport orbs, yellow/pink/red/blue pads, slopes, moving/orbiting platforms, practice checkpoints, groups/layers, multiselect editor tools, Move/Rotate/Spawn/Camera triggers, metadata, and optional custom music.

## Large systems deferred

Dual Mode, Platformer Mode, complete 2.2 trigger/event language, keyframes, advanced camera behaviors, shader/post-processing stack, full arbitrary rotated-surface collision, and online community level infrastructure.

No Geometry Dash copyrighted levels, music, artwork, logos, or branding are included.
